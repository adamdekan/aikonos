Aikonos lets you talk to AI agents that can do real work: read and write files, create
documents, search the web, and reach into connected drives like Google Drive and OneDrive.
An agent is not a chatbot that only answers questions. It plans a sequence of actions, asks
for the tools it needs, and reports back what it did.

Every action an agent takes is checked against permissions your organization has set, at the
moment the action happens. If your account or group has not been granted a tool, the agent
cannot use it on your behalf, no matter what you ask for. Some actions run right away once
that check passes. Riskier ones, destructive changes or actions outside your usual pattern,
pause and ask you, or an administrator, to approve them first. Every decision, allowed or
denied, is written to an audit trail your organization's administrators can review.

This section explains what these pieces are: agents and how they handle tasks, the skills and
tools an agent can use, workflows that turn a proven sequence of steps into something
reusable, your personal workspace and files, connections to outside services, delegating
tasks to teammates, and how governance and audit work behind the scenes.

## Who this site is for

If you use Aikonos to get work done, chatting with an agent, running workflows, managing your
files, read the concept pages here first, then the [User Guide](/guides/) for the
screen-by-screen detail.

If you administer Aikonos for your organization, managing who can use which tools, setting
spend limits, reviewing the audit trail, the concepts still apply, and the
[Administrator Guide](/admin/) covers every admin screen and setting.
