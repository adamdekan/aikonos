Personal settings covers everything about how Aikonos looks and behaves for you personally, as
opposed to anything your organization configures for everyone. Open it from the gear icon
next to your name in the sidebar footer.

## Account

The Account tab shows your display name and email, both read-only since they come from your
organization's sign-in. It also has a toggle, "Show broker governance details in chat," which
controls whether extra governance detail is visible alongside your conversations. See
[Governance & audit](/concepts/governance-and-audit/) for what that detail describes.

## Appearance

The Appearance tab is a theme choice: Dark, Light, or System. System follows your operating
system's light/dark setting live, so it switches automatically if you change it at the OS
level while Aikonos is open.

## Chat

The Chat tab controls how your conversation history is handled on this device:

- **Persist chat history on this device** turns local storage of your sessions on or off.
- **Turns to keep**, a number from 5 to 200, controls how much of each conversation is kept
  in that local storage. It is disabled while persistence is off.
- **Agent instructions**, a textarea capped at 2000 characters with a live counter, adds your
  own instructions to every new chat you start on this device.

:::note
These preferences are per-browser and per-device, not tied to your account across devices.
Agent instructions you set here apply only to new chats started afterward; a chat already in
progress keeps the instructions it started with.
:::

## Signing out

The modal's footer has Sign out, which ends your session and returns you to your
organization's sign-in page, and Close, which closes the settings modal without signing you
out.
