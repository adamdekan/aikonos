Aikonos for Windows is the Aikonos console as an app on your PC. It connects to the same
Aikonos server as the web console, shows the same screens (Home, Chat, Files, Connections,
Schedules, Workflows, My Skills, Inbox and your settings) and works on the same
conversations and files, so you can switch between the app and the browser at any time.
What you can do is decided by your organization exactly as in the browser.

Your organization provides the app; ask your administrator where to get it.

## Signing in

The first time you open the app, enter the address of your organization's Aikonos server,
the same address you use in the browser, and choose **Sign in**. Your browser opens your
organization's sign-in page; once you have signed in there, the app comes back to the front.
If you closed the browser before finishing, choose **Open the browser again**.

The app remembers the server address, but not your sign-in: you sign in again each time you
start it. If your browser is still signed in to your organization, that takes a moment. If
your organization set the app up with its server's address, it skips the address and goes
straight to your sign-in page.

## Files from your PC

The agent can't see the files on your PC. A file reaches Aikonos only when you send it:

- **In a chat**, use the attach button in the composer (**Attach files from this PC**), or
  drag files onto it. Each file is uploaded to your workspace (pictures go into the
  `references` folder) and mentioned in your message, so the agent works with it there.
- **In Files**, use **Upload**, or drag files onto the list, or onto a folder to upload into it.

Files are limited to 10 MiB each, as in the browser.

To keep a copy of a workspace file on your PC, open **Files** and choose the download icon
(**Save to this PC**) on the file. You pick where it goes, and the app then shows it in File
Explorer.

## Approvals

When an agent asks for your approval, the app shows the same approval dialog as the web
console. Closing it, or pressing Esc, denies the request. If the app is in the background, a
Windows notification tells you an approval is waiting.

## Updates

Your server tells the app which version it expects. When a newer one is available, an
**Update to …** link appears at the bottom of the sidebar, and **Settings → Account** shows the
new version with a **Download** button. If your server needs a newer version than yours, the
app says so when you sign in and offers the download.

## What stays on your PC

The app keeps only your preferences on the PC: the server address, the theme, the sidebar
layout, your chat instructions, and the folders you last used to open or save files.
Conversations stay in your workspace on the server, and the app forgets your sign-in when it
closes.
