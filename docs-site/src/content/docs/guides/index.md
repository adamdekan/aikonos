This section documents every screen and control you see as a Aikonos user: signing in, the
Home screen, chatting with agents, the files explorer, connections, schedules, workflows,
your inbox, and personal settings. Each page after this one covers a single screen and walks
through its controls in the order they appear.

## Signing in

Aikonos signs you in through your organization's own sign-in page, not a separate Aikonos
account. Once you authenticate, what you can see and do inside Aikonos depends entirely on
what your administrator has granted your account or the groups you belong to. See
[Governance & audit](/concepts/governance-and-audit/) for how that permission checking
works.

## The Home screen

After you sign in, Aikonos lands you on the Home screen. It greets you by name and shows a
quick-start composer: a text box asking "How can I help you today?" Type a request and press
Enter, or press the Send button, to route straight into a new chat with that request already
sent.

Below the composer are five topic chips: Write, Learn, Code, Life stuff, and From Drive.
Clicking one pre-fills a starter prompt for that topic and submits it right away, so you land
in chat with a conversation already under way.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is where you move between every part of Aikonos:

- **New chat** clears your current conversation and starts a fresh one.
- **Workspace nav** lists Chat, Files, Connections, Schedules, Workflows, and Inbox. Which of
  these you see depends on what your organization has granted you: Connections only appears
  if your organization has at least one connector provider configured, and Schedules and
  Workflows only appear if you have been granted the matching permission. An item's absence
  from the sidebar is a convenience, not the actual security boundary. The underlying action
  is still checked on the server every time, whether or not you can see a menu item for it.
- **Per-agent entries** appear below the workspace nav, one row for every agent you are
  directly assigned to. See [Agents & tasks](/concepts/agents-and-tasks/) for what an agent
  is.
- **Session history** lists your past chat sessions below the nav, with a filter box for
  finding one by name and a toggle for showing only scheduled sessions.
- A **collapse toggle** near the top of the sidebar switches it to a narrow, icon-only rail
  when you want more room for the main screen.
- The **footer** shows your display name and a gear icon. The gear opens your personal
  settings; see the [Personal settings guide](/guides/personal-settings/) for every tab
  inside it.
